Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

01Do you process visas?

Yes — we can assist with the full visa process for the routes we run.

02Are meals included in the tour?

We can organize meals throughout the trip on request — just let us know when booking.

03What documents do I need for the trip?

For Russia tours: a valid passport. For Central Asia: an international passport valid for at least 6 months, plus a visa if required. We'll help prepare everything.

04Can the itinerary be changed?

Always. Tell us what you're after and we'll adapt the program to fit your interests and pace.

05What happens if the weather turns bad?

We always keep a backup plan — museums, covered attractions, or a route better suited to the conditions.
