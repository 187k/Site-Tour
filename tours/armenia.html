Armenia - Cradle of Christianity

Journey through one of the world's oldest civilizations with ancient monasteries and mountain lakes

Overview

Armenia, one of the world's oldest nations, is a treasure trove of ancient monasteries, dramatic landscapes, and rich cultural heritage. From the vibrant capital Yerevan to the serene Lake Sevan and the legendary Tatev Monastery, this country offers profound historical and natural beauty. This 6-day tour explores the heart of the South Caucasus.

Highlights

  • Yerevan - pink city capital
  • Lake Sevan and Sevanavank Monastery
  • Tatev Monastery and Wings of Tatev
  • Garni Temple - Hellenistic monument
  • Geghard Monastery - carved in rock
  • Armenian brandy tasting
